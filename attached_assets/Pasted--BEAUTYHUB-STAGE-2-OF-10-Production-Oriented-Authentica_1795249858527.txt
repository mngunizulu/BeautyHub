## 9. Cancellation-policy foundations

Prepare an extensible, database-driven cancellation-policy model.

Initial default business policy:

- Free cancellation until 24 hours before the appointment.
- Cancellation inside that period: configured fee of 100% of the booked service total.
- No-show: configured fee of 100% of the booked service total.

These values must be configurable policy data, not hardcoded application logic.

The future policy engine must support:

- Different notice periods.
- Percentage-based fees.
- Fixed fees.
- No-show rules.
- Waivers.
- Exceptions.
- Policy versions.
- Customer acceptance records.
- Historical policy snapshots.

Allow business owners to view and configure basic cancellation-policy settings, subject to platform controls.

Document that real-money enforcement requires legal review and valid customer payment authorization.

Do not implement actual fee collection in Stage 2.

Do not claim that a 100% cancellation fee is automatically legally enforceable in Germany.

## 10. Payment-method foundations

BeautyHub will eventually require registered customers to save a payment card before confirming appointments.

The intended future booking behavior is:

1. Customer selects one or more services.
2. Customer chooses an available time.
3. Customer signs in.
4. Customer securely provides a payment card through a payment provider.
5. Customer accepts the applicable cancellation policy.
6. Customer confirms the booking.
7. No service fee is charged at booking time.
8. The service is charged after completion or a valid cancellation/no-show charge is processed according to the accepted terms and payment rules.

For Stage 2:

- Design the payment-provider abstraction.
- Prepare the customer payment-method association model.
- Establish secure authorization boundaries.
- Document the required future setup-intent and off-session payment workflows.
- Ensure the data model never requires raw card data.
- Do not store card numbers or CVV.
- Do not create fake production payment credentials.
- Do not build misleading card-entry fields that appear to save real payment cards.

Stripe SetupIntents or an equivalent provider may be considered in the later payment integration stage.

Until the provider is configured, display only clearly labeled development placeholders where necessary.

Actual card-on-file setup, booking payment authorization, and fee collection belong to later stages.
